# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers checking Claude, Codex and Gemini usage while coding, without opening a large dashboard. Windows first; Mac integration remains future work.

## Product Purpose

A thin, unobtrusive edge notch with real provider readings, hover details and accessible settings. Preserve CodeNotch's original folding and hit-region behaviour.

## Brand Personality

Measured, technical, restrained. The user explicitly chose dydxfx.com as the visual authority and CodeNotch as the interaction reference.

## Anti-references

The user rejected the large, permanently expanded Electron preview. Avoid an oversized panel, approximate branding and settings-only theming. Light and dark must include the notch itself.

## Design Principles

- Remain small until needed.
- Carry the same theme through settings, rings and details.
- Use the live website's brand tokens, not guessed substitutes.
- Keep real readings distinguishable from unavailable readings.
- Glass is a requested material treatment, not justification for reducing legibility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve existing reduced-motion support, keyboard-operable settings and readable labels. No additional user-specific accessibility requirements have been supplied. Do not claim WCAG certification.
